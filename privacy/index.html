Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

On-device photo processing

Watermark Remover detects, learns, and removes watermarks from your photos entirely on your Android device. Source photos, learned watermark templates, and result photos are never uploaded to any server for processing. You select a photo through Android's system picker or share action; the app receives access to that selected file rather than broad read access to your media library.

Optional reports

The app uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication and Firebase Firestore (collection reports) for feedback, crash reports, out-of-memory reports, and Hardware Test reports. Each of these can be disabled in Settings, and sending is always your choice except where noted.

  • Feedback: your typed message, app version, device model, Android version, a build fingerprint, the processing engine in use, a redacted log snippet, and the app's current display language. If you tick "Image details" before sending, it may also include the current screen, current settings, and facts about the photos involved in the current session (count, dimensions, MIME types, byte sizes) and about the watermark template (detection score, quality, cleaned fraction, whether it was judged periodic, source-image count, and how many images were dropped for budget). No photo pixel data is ever included.
  • Optional screenshot: feedback may include a screenshot you choose to attach, stored in Firebase Storage under reports/screenshots/<reportId>.png. The local screenshot cache expires after 30 days.
  • Crash and out-of-memory reports: a stack trace or thread name, and for an out-of-memory kill, facts the operating system records about the kill (importance, memory usage, description, timestamp). No image data. Automatic sharing can be disabled in Settings.
  • Hardware Test reports: device facts (model, manufacturer, chipset, Android version, RAM, app version) and timing/prediction measurements from the optional on-device hardware check. No image data of any kind. Off by default; enabled only via its own Settings toggle.

Every report also includes the app's currently-displayed language as a BCP-47 tag, and, when it differs, your device's requested and default languages.

Identifiers

No user account is required. Firebase creates an anonymous app-account identifier (uid). Reports also include Android ID so related submissions can be grouped. Neither is used as an advertising identifier or intentionally tied to your name or email.

Advertising and analytics

The app does not include advertising SDKs, usage-analytics SDKs, or crash-analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, no Crashlytics — verified against the app's build configuration). Google Play may separately process store-level data under Google's own privacy policies.

Storage and retention

Submitted reports are stored in Firebase Firestore; optional screenshots are stored in Firebase Storage. A fixed server-side retention period is not currently published.

Your controls

  • Disable crash and out-of-memory reporting in Settings.
  • Disable Hardware Test reporting in Settings.
  • Review feedback fields, including the "Image details" checkbox, and remove an optional screenshot before sending.
